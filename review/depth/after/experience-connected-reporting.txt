Connecting data collection and reporting.

A Workiva implementation I led at Citrin Cooperman, from integration through user acceptance testing, go-live, and executive sign-off.

Manager of Digital Services, Workiva · December 2024–October 2025

The scope

Connect the customer’s system of record with a data-collection and reporting workflow in Workiva.

The implementation included a bidirectional API integration, single sign-on, and an audit trail.

Client identity and implementation materials are not public. This is an engagement summary, not a live client demonstration.

My responsibility

I led the implementation and user acceptance testing through go-live and executive sign-off. In my role at Citrin Cooperman, I also managed a five-person Workiva implementation team, coached consultants, resolved customer escalations, and led testing and training.

This was delivery leadership across the technical work and the people using it—not simply configuration of a reporting tool.

What had to come together
Bidirectional integration
Connecting Workiva and the customer’s system of record through an API.
Access and traceability
Single sign-on and an audit trail within the implementation scope.
Acceptance and launch
User acceptance testing, go-live, and executive sign-off.
The delivery lesson

For me, an integration is not the whole implementation. It also needs a customer who understands the workflow, a team prepared to support it, and a clear acceptance process.

That is the connection between my technical and consulting work: making the system usable, explaining it to the people responsible for it, and carrying the engagement through launch.
