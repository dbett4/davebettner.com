I’m Dave Bettner, based in Des Moines, Iowa. I work with finance teams, consultants, and technical teams to make reporting systems usable.

From reporting to implementation.

I started at Workiva in 2015, supporting public-company SEC reporting and XBRL. That work meant managing review cycles and helping customers meet filing deadlines.

Later roles took me into healthcare integrations, Workiva solution architecture, and accounting-firm delivery leadership. Today, I’m a Senior Manager at LSL, leading Workiva delivery for city and county finance teams.

Building as part of the job.

I develop Python tools for reporting links, formulas, and validation checks. I’m also exploring AI-assisted accounting workflows through personal projects that make their inputs, approval requirements, and failure cases inspectable.

The part I keep coming back to is helping people understand a system well enough to use it—and finding what needs to change when they can’t.
