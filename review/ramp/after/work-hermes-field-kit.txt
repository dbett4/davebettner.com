All projects

Independent project using synthetic cases. Not an official Nous or Hermes product, customer deployment, or accepted policy decision.

The problem

An agent completing a task is not enough to establish that the task met the organization’s requirements.

What I built

I built policy configurations, a version-specific capability map, negative tests, and an offline evaluation workflow. The final decision remains with a person.

What you can inspect

The public repository documents the offline checks and known gaps. A recorded live trial remains unaccepted; it is not presented as a completed customer result.
